Questions

Is StudySpace free for students?

Yes. There is a free tier covering timetable, attendance, lectures, notes and focus sessions.

Do web and Android stay in sync?

Yes. Changes made on one device appear on your others without a manual reload.

Does the Android app work offline?

The app keeps a local cache, so your timetable and recent data are available without a connection.

What can I keep in the document vault?

Syllabus PDFs, lecture slides, lab manuals and past question papers, organized by subject.
